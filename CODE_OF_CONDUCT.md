# Code of conduct

Unmask is a project about protecting people. We want contributing to it to feel safe too.

## Our pledge

We, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to security or to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's real emails, addresses or personal data in issues, pull requests or tests.
- Credit others' work.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing working phishing kits, live malicious links, or stolen credentials. Use defanged samples (`hxxps://evil[.]top`) and fictional data.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/praneeth132006), or use a private security advisory if it involves a security matter. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct. Depending on severity, they may give a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project.
